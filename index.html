Who Lives Where in Armenia: A Look at the 2021 Voter Register

An interactive look at 2.58 million registered voters by region, age, birth date and first name. Hover over any chart for exact values, and use the controls to move through years and decades.

Voters by region

Yerevan accounts for about a third of all records, more than the next three regions combined.

Under 50 vs. 50 and over, by region

Yerevan, Lori and Tavush are the only regions where voters aged 50+ outnumber those under 50.

Voters by birth year

The register peaks for people born in 1990, then falls steadily through the 1990s as births dropped after independence. 2003 is the youngest cohort, and only part of it was old enough to be listed.

Births by month

Pick a birth year, or view all years combined. January is inflated in the combined view because "1 January" is often recorded when the exact birth date is unknown.

How the 20 most common names rose and fell (1930–2003)

Each cell is that name's share of all voters born in that year. Darker blue means more popular. Anahit fades after the 1960s and Armen after the 1980s, while Ani, Mariam and Lilit take off from the 1980s on.

Source: Armenia 2021 voter register (shnik_2021), 2,577,169 records, analysed in the dataviz-2 notebook. Ages are as of 2026. Region counts exclude 20 records with no region. Names are shown in Armenian with a Latin transliteration.
